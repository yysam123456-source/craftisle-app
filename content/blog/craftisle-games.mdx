Craftisle Games brings free, instantly playable HTML5 games to your browser. No downloads, no installations, no signups — just click and play.

## Available Games

### Mykos Island Builder

Build your dream island in this creative sandbox game. Inspired by the Greek island of Mykonos, you'll design landscapes, construct buildings, and watch your island come to life.

**What you can do:**
- **Terrain sculpting** — Raise mountains, carve valleys, shape coastlines
- **Building placement** — Houses, lighthouses, windmills, and more
- **Vegetation** — Plant trees, flowers, and gardens
- **Path networks** — Connect your island with roads and trails
- **Dynamic water** — Realistic water edges that adapt to your terrain

**Why it's special:** Mykos Island Builder isn't just a game — it's a creative tool. Use it as a visual brainstorming platform for:
- Landscape architecture concepts
- World-building for stories and D&D campaigns
- Relaxation and mindfulness through creative play
- Teaching basic geography and urban planning concepts

### Tiny World Builder

Create miniature worlds with endless creative possibilities:

- **Modular building system** — Snap together pre-designed pieces or free-build
- **Multiple biomes** — Forest, desert, arctic, tropical, and urban themes
- **Character placement** — Populate your world with tiny inhabitants
- **Day/night cycle** — Watch your world change with dynamic lighting
- **Export & share** — Save your creation as an image to share with friends
