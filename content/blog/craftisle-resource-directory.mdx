"If it's free and useful for developers, it should be findable."

That thought led to the Craftisle Resource Directory. We pulled data from four well-maintained community repositories, deduplicated entries, and built a searchable index.

## Where the data comes from

→ [Browse AI Tools](/directory/Artificial-Intelligence) | [View SaaS Free Tiers](/directory/DevOps-Cloud) | [Self-Hosted Alternatives](/directory/Privacy-Security)

- **FMHY** — 6,187 resources across 14 categories. The largest collection. Covers AI tools, media tools, privacy resources, educational content, and more.
- **free-for-dev** — 1,232 SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS tools with free tiers. If a startup offers a free plan for developers, it's probably listed here.
- **awesome-selfhosted** — 1,176 self-hosted alternatives to popular services. Want to run your own Google Drive or Slack alternative? Start here.
- **public-apis** — 1,534 free public APIs. Weather, finance, government data, space data, animal facts — if there's a free API, this list catalogs it.

Total: 10,129 resources across 205 unique categories.

## How the directory works

The directory is static-generated. Each resource detail page is an SSG route pre-rendered at build time with a 6-hour ISR revalidation window.

You can browse by category, search by name or description, and filter by source (FMHY, free-for-dev, etc.). Favorites are stored in localStorage.

## What the directory isn't

- **Not a live index.** Data updates when we rebuild the site with fresh source data. Resources added to FMHY this morning won't appear until the next sync.
- **Not everything is verified.** Some links may be dead or outdated. Community-maintained lists have a known decay rate — roughly 5-10% of links break annually.
- **Not all free forever.** Services listed as "free" may have changed their pricing. Always check the current terms before depending on something.

## Why four sources?

Monolithic "best of" lists rot fast. Community-maintained repos (with active PRs and issues) stay fresher. By pulling from four active sources, the directory covers different angles: FMHY for discovery, free-for-dev for SaaS, awesome-selfhosted for self-hosting, public-apis for data.
