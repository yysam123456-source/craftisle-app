The pomodoro timer on Craftisle started as a personal tool. It grew into a collection of small utilities that do one thing and don't try to be more.

## The tools

→ [Try Pomodoro Timer](/tools/pomodoro) | [Open Stopwatch](/tools/stopwatch) | [Coin Flip](/tools/coin-flip)

### Pomodoro Timer

25-minute work intervals, 5-minute breaks, 15-minute long breaks after 4 cycles. Buttons for start, pause, reset. Browser notification when a session ends.

No account sync, no statistics dashboard, no task list. Just a timer. If you need session logging and analytics, use a dedicated app like Toggl. If you need a timer that works in a browser tab, this is it.

### Stopwatch

Start, pause, lap, reset. Displays milliseconds, seconds, minutes. Good for timing presentations, cooking, workouts — anything where you need a running clock.

### Countdown Timer

Set hours/minutes/seconds, start, it counts down. Alarm sound at zero. For "I need to leave in 45 minutes" — the most boring and most useful kind of tool.

### Coin Flip

Heads or tails. Uses `Math.random()` — not cryptographically secure. Don't use it to settle billion-dollar contracts. Do use it to decide who buys lunch.

### Wheel Spinner

Customizable prize wheel. Add text labels, spin, it lands on one. Use `Crypto.getRandomValues()` for the selection. Popular for giveaways and team activity picks.

### Scoreboard

Add players, increment/decrement scores, reset. Two teams or individual mode. For keeping score in board game nights or office ping-pong.

### Text to Speech

Paste text, pick a voice (uses the browser's SpeechSynthesis API), click play. Voice quality depends on your OS — macOS has the best built-in voices. Windows voices sound more robotic.
