Craftisle's utility tools are designed for everyday productivity — timers, counters, and decision-making helpers that you can reach for in seconds. Every tool works offline after initial page load.

## All 7 Utility Tools

### 1. Pomodoro Timer

A full Pomodoro Technique implementation with customizable intervals:

- **Default cycle:** 25 min work → 5 min short break → repeat 4× → 15 min long break
- **Customizable durations** — Adjust work, short break, and long break lengths
- **Session tracking** — Count completed pomodoros per day
- **Desktop notifications** — Alert even when the tab is in the background
- **Audio alerts** — Distinct sounds for work start, break start, and cycle complete

**The science:** The Pomodoro Technique leverages your brain's ultradian rhythm by breaking work into manageable 25-minute chunks. After 4 cycles (about 2 hours), a longer break prevents mental fatigue.

### 2. Stopwatch

A high-precision stopwatch using `performance.now()` for microsecond accuracy:

- **Millisecond display** — HH:MM:SS.mmm format
- **Lap timing** — Record up to 99 split times without stopping
- **Background timing** — Keeps running even when you switch tabs (Web Worker API)
- **Fullscreen mode** — Large display for presentations or sports timing

**Use cases:** Athletic timing, productivity measurement, science experiments, cooking competitions.

### 3. Countdown Timer

Set custom countdowns for events, deadlines, or timed activities:

- **Flexible input** — Set hours, minutes, and seconds independently
- **Visual progress bar** — See remaining time at a glance
- **Alarm with tab flash** — Impossible to miss when time is up
- **Preset durations** — Quick-set buttons for 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 60 minutes

### 4. Coin Flip

A virtual coin toss using cryptographic randomness (`Crypto.getRandomValues()`):

- **True 50/50 probability** — No pseudo-random bias
- **Realistic animation** — Coin spin with satisfying visual feedback
- **Statistics tracking** — Heads/tails count and percentage over time
- **Sound effect** — Optional coin flip audio

### 5. Wheel Spinner

A customizable prize wheel for random selections:

- **Add custom entries** — Name your own wheel segments
- **Remove/Edit entries** — Modify the wheel on the fly
- **Smooth spin animation** — With easing for dramatic reveals
- **History tracking** — See previous spin results

**Perfect for:** Classroom activities, giveaway drawings, team assignment randomization, decision making with more than 2 options.

### 6. Scoreboard

A digital score counter for games and competitions:

- **Multiple players/teams** — Add as many as you need
- **Increment/Decrement** — Adjust scores easily
- **Custom labels** — Name your players or teams
- **Reset all** — Quick reset for new games

### 7. Counter

A simple tally counter with persistent storage:

- **Increment/Decrement** — Click buttons or use arrow keys
- **Custom step size** — Count by 1, 5, 10, or any number
- **Session persistence** — Counter value saved in localStorage
- **Large display** — Easy to read from a distance

**Use cases:** Gym rep tracking, inventory counting, event attendance, knitting row counting.

## Productivity Stack

Combine these tools for a complete productivity workflow:

1. **Start a Pomodoro** (25 min focus session)
2. **Use the Stopwatch** to track how long specific tasks take
3. **Set a Countdown** for meeting timeboxes
4. **Track completed Pomodoros** with the Counter
5. **Use Coin Flip or Wheel** for break-time decisions
