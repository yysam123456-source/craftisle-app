## What it's not

- **Not a design tool.** If you want Canva-style visual resumes with custom fonts and graphics, use Canva. This builder prioritizes ATS readability over visual flair.
- **No cloud backup.** Your resume lives in localStorage. Clear your browser data, lose your resume. Export frequently.
- **No AI writing.** No GPT-generated bullet points. No keyword optimization. You write the content.

## Why local storage matters

If you upload your resume to a "free" builder that stores it on their servers, you're trusting them with your full work history, education, and contact info. If they get breached, that data is public. With localStorage, your data never leaves your machine.

The trade-off: you're responsible for backups. Export a PDF and DOCX after each edit.
