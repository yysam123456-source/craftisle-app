The Craftisle Resume Builder ([resume.craftisle.com](https://resume.craftisle.com)) is a privacy-first, feature-rich resume creation tool built on Reactive Resume. Unlike most resume builders, every piece of data stays in your browser's localStorage — no account, no cloud storage, no data collection.

## Why a Resume Builder on Craftisle?

Most online resume builders require you to create an account, upload your data to their servers, and often lock exporting behind a paywall. Craftisle's approach is fundamentally different:

- **Zero data collection** — Your resume data never leaves your browser
- **No account required** — Start building immediately
- **Free PDF/DOCX export** — No watermarks, no page limits, no paywalls
- **Full offline support** — Edit your resume without internet after initial load

## Key Features

- **Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough**

- **Bullet and numbered lists**
- **Hyperlinks** — Link to your portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn

### Export Options

- **PDF export** — Print-ready, ATS-optimized PDFs with embedded fonts

### Privacy Architecture

The Resume Builder's privacy model is built on localStorage:

1. All resume data is stored in your browser's localStorage
2. No cookies, no tracking scripts, no analytics on your data
3. You can export your data as JSON and import it on another device
4. Clear your browser data, and your resume data is gone — no server copies exist

## Tips for an Effective Resume

1. **Tailor for ATS** — Use keywords from the job description in your skills and experience sections
2. **Quantify achievements** — "Increased revenue by 30%" beats "Responsible for revenue growth"
3. **Keep it concise** — Aim for 1 page (early career) or 2 pages (10+ years experience)
4. **Use action verbs** — "Designed", "Implemented", "Led", "Optimized"
5. **Proofread** — Use the Text Diff tool to compare versions after edits
