## Why "Client-Side" Is the Killer Feature

The fundamental difference between Craftisle and most alternatives is the processing architecture:

**Server-side processing (most competitors):**
1. You upload your file to their server
2. Their server processes it
3. You download the result
4. (They may or may not delete your file)

**Client-side processing (Craftisle):**
1. Your browser reads the file locally
2. JavaScript/WebAssembly processes it in your browser's memory
3. The result is saved directly to your downloads

This matters because:
- **NDAs and confidential documents** — You can't upload a legal contract to a random server
- **GDPR/HIPAA compliance** — Client-side processing simplifies compliance
- **Large files** — No upload wait time for multi-GB files
- **Offline use** — Tools work without internet after initial load
