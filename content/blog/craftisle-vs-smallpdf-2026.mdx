## Round 3: Privacy & Security

### SmallPDF

**How it works**:
1. You upload your PDF to **SmallPDF's servers**
2. SmallPDF processes it (compress, merge, convert)
3. You download the result

**Privacy risks**:
- ❌ Your PDF is **on their server** (even if deleted after 1 hour)
- ❌ **GDPR compliance** (but they still have your data)
- ❌ **No end-to-end encryption** (they can technically read your PDF)

### Craftisle (pdfcraft)

**How it works**:
1. You open [pdfcraft.app](https://pdfcraft.app)
2. You select a PDF (file stays in your browser)
3. **pdf-lib** (JavaScript library) processes it **client-side**
4. You download the result (never left your device)

**Privacy advantages**:
- ✅ **No upload** — file never leaves your browser
- ✅ **No server** — nothing to hack
- ✅ **No GDPR compliance needed** (no data collected)
- ✅ **End-to-end privacy** (even Craftisle can't see your PDF)

**Winner**: **Craftisle** (100% private, no upload)

## FAQ

### Is Craftisle (pdfcraft) really 100% free?

**Yes**. No signup, no watermark, no credit card, no daily limits.

### Is SmallPDF safe to use?

**Mostly**. They're GDPR-compliant and delete files after 1 hour. But your PDF **is uploaded to their server** — if you're handling sensitive documents, use Craftisle (client-side).

### Which is better for large PDFs (100MB+)?

**SmallPDF**. Their server-side processing handles large files better. Craftisle (pdfcraft) uses **pdf-lib** (runs in browser), which might be slow for very large PDFs.

### Can I use Craftisle offline?

**Not yet**. Both tools require an internet connection. Service worker support is on Craftisle's roadmap.

### Does Craftisle have an API?

**Not yet**. SmallPDF has an API (for developers). Craftisle is **browser-based only** (no server, no API).

## Conclusion: Which Should You Use?

### Use **SmallPDF** if:
- You need to process **very large PDFs** (100MB+)
- You need an **API** (for integrating into your app)
- You don't mind **paying $12/month** for unlimited access

### Use **Craftisle (pdfcraft)** if:
- You want **free, unlimited** PDF tools (no daily limits)
- You care about **privacy** (no upload, 100% client-side)
- You don't want to **sign up** or **enter credit card**
- You're processing **moderate-sized PDFs** (&lt;50MB)

### Our recommendation:

**For most users**: Use **Craftisle (pdfcraft)** — it's free, private, and has no limits.

**For enterprises**: SmallPDF might be better (API access, larger file support).
