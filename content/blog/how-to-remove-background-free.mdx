## Tips for Best Results

### ✅ Do This:

1. **Use high-contrast images**: Subject should be clearly different from background.
   - Good: Person in front of solid-color wall.
   - Bad: Person in front of cluttered room (similar colors to clothes).

2. **Use good lighting**: Shadows on face/object make it harder for AI to detect edges.
   - Good: Even lighting, no harsh shadows.
   - Bad: Backlit subject, harsh shadows.

3. **Use high resolution**: 1000px+ wide images give AI more detail to work with.
   - Good: 4000x3000px photo from DSLR.
   - Bad: 300x200px thumbnail.

### ❌ Don't Do This:

1. **Don't use blurry images**: AI can't detect edges if they're blurry.
2. **Don't use images with similar colors**: If subject and background are same color, AI will struggle.
3. **Don't use images with complex backgrounds**: Crowded scenes with many objects confuse AI.
